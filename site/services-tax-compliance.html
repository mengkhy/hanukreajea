Cambodia Business Tax Compliance Services

Monthly and annual tax compliance in Cambodia — VAT returns, corporate income tax, and GDT filings managed so your business stays fully compliant

GDT Filings & Tax Advisory

Cambodia's tax system requires businesses to file multiple monthly returns, maintain meticulous records, and meet strict deadlines set by the General Department of Taxation. Missing a filing date or miscalculating tax obligations can result in significant penalties, interest charges, and audit exposure.

Bodiatrade provides comprehensive tax compliance services that cover your full obligations—from monthly prepayment of income tax and VAT returns to annual corporate income tax filings and withholding tax submissions. Our tax specialists keep your business permanently current with the GDT, so you never face avoidable penalties.

Tax Compliance Services

Full-spectrum tax compliance management for businesses operating under Cambodia's tax regime.

Monthly Tax Returns

Timely preparation and submission of monthly prepayment of income tax, salary tax, and VAT returns. We collect your monthly financial data, prepare accurate returns, and submit to the GDT before each deadline.

Annual Income Tax Filing

Corporate income tax calculation and annual return submission, including reconciliation of monthly prepayments against actual liability. We ensure your annual filing accurately reflects your business's financial position.

VAT Registration & Management

VAT registration with the GDT, guidance on VAT-compliant invoicing practices, and monthly VAT return reporting. We advise on input tax credits and ensure your VAT position is correctly calculated each month.

Withholding Tax

Accurate calculation of withholding tax on payments to suppliers, contractors, and service providers, with timely monthly submission to the GDT. We ensure correct rates are applied to each payment category under Cambodian tax law.

Tax Planning & Advisory

Legal tax optimization strategies tailored to your business structure. We provide guidance on Qualified Investment Project (QIP) status, Special Economic Zone (SEZ) benefits, and other incentive frameworks available in Cambodia.

Tax Audit Support

Comprehensive representation and documentation support during GDT tax audits. We organize your records, liaise directly with tax officers, and work to achieve the most favorable outcome for your business.
