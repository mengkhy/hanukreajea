General Information

Hanukreajea offers three primary service lines: (1) Business Consulting — covering company registration, legal compliance, and strategic planning; (2) Agricultural Trade — import and export solutions for quality Cambodian products; and (3) Trade & Partnership Development — connecting businesses with reliable regional partners.

Hanukreajea is headquartered in Phnom Penh, Cambodia's capital city. Our strategic location in the business district provides convenient access to government agencies, ports, and regional trading partners.

To start a project, simply reach out to us via our contact form or email. We will schedule an initial consultation to assess your needs, followed by a formal proposal and a customized project roadmap.

While we specialize deeply in Agricultural Trade, we also provide business consulting for a wide range of sectors including Logistics, Manufacturing, Retail, and Distribution across Cambodia and ASEAN.
