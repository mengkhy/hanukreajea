Singapore F&B Group: Full Company Registration in Cambodia in 38 Days

A Singapore-headquartered food and beverage company had identified a Phnom Penh location and signed a letter of intent with a local property developer. Their regional expansion timeline gave them a strict 45-day window to achieve full legal operational status in Cambodia — or forfeit the lease opportunity.

Bodiatrade was engaged to manage the entire company registration process in Cambodia. Our team coordinated the Ministry of Commerce (MOC) business registration, General Department of Taxation (GDT) tax identification number, and patent (business licence) applications in parallel rather than sequentially — compressing a process that typically takes 6–8 weeks into under 40 days.

We also handled Social Security Fund (NSSF) registration for the client's initial five staff and prepared the full suite of corporate documentation required by their Singapore parent company's legal team for cross-border compliance reporting.

Outcome: The company received its Certificate of Incorporation, Tax Registration Certificate, and business licence within 38 days. The lease was secured and the Phnom Penh outlet opened on schedule. The client has since engaged Bodiatrade for ongoing monthly tax compliance and HR support.

Cambodian Manufacturer: GDT Audit Resolved, Zero Penalties

A Cambodian garment accessories manufacturer had grown quickly over three years, but its internal accounting had not kept pace. When the company received a General Department of Taxation (GDT) audit notice, its management team discovered that monthly VAT returns had been filed inconsistently and withholding tax on contractor payments had not been properly tracked for two fiscal years.

Bodiatrade's tax compliance team conducted a full review of the company's historical filings, identified the discrepancies, and prepared a voluntary disclosure package for submission to the GDT ahead of the scheduled audit date. We also prepared reconciled financial records, corrected prior period returns, and briefed the company's director on what to expect during the audit process.

During the audit itself, our consultants attended alongside the client's management to respond to examiner queries and provide supporting documentation in real time. The audit was completed in a single session.

Outcome: The GDT audit was closed without financial penalty. The tax authority accepted the voluntary disclosure and corrected filings. Bodiatrade subsequently implemented a monthly compliance calendar and took over the company's GDT filing obligations, ensuring no further discrepancies have occurred in the 18 months since.

Cambodian Cashew Processor: First ASEAN Export in 9 Weeks

A family-owned cashew processing operation in Kampong Thom province had been supplying the Phnom Penh domestic market for four years and wanted to expand into regional export — specifically targeting buyers in Vietnam and Thailand. Their first attempted shipment had been held at the border due to missing phytosanitary certificates and incorrect customs documentation, resulting in spoilage costs and a lost buyer relationship.

Bodiatrade's agricultural trade team conducted a full export readiness assessment. We identified three documentation gaps: the company lacked a CAMCONTROL quality certification, had no established relationship with a licensed freight forwarder, and was using an incorrect HS code classification for processed cashew products — a common error that triggers customs holds.

We managed the CAMCONTROL application process, corrected the HS code classification, and connected the client with a Phnom Penh-based freight forwarder experienced in ASEAN agricultural shipments. We also prepared a complete export documentation pack — phytosanitary certificate, certificate of origin, commercial invoice, and packing list templates — that the client's team could reuse for future shipments.

Outcome: The first compliant export shipment was dispatched to a Vietnamese buyer within 9 weeks of engagement. A second buyer in Thailand was identified through Bodiatrade's trade network and a supply agreement was signed three months later. The client now exports two containers per month with zero customs delays.
