Agricultural Trade Solutions

Facilitating agricultural import and export from Cambodia to regional and global markets — with expertise, integrity, and sustainable practices.

Import • Export • Trade Solutions

Cambodia's agricultural sector is a vital engine of the economy, producing world-class rice, pepper, cashew nuts, and fresh produce. Yet accessing regional and global markets requires navigating complex international standards, regulatory requirements, and logistics challenges.

Bodiatrade facilitates seamless agricultural import and export operations. We connect Cambodian producers with buyers across ASEAN, Asia-Pacific, and the European Union. Our integrated approach—combining trade facilitation, quality assurance, supply chain management, and comprehensive documentation—ensures your products meet international standards while reaching the right markets at the right time. Whether you're a producer looking to export or an importer sourcing quality agricultural products, we deliver end-to-end solutions that drive profitability and growth.

Our Trade Services

From production to distribution, we manage every aspect of agricultural trade.

Import & Export Facilitation

End-to-end management of cross-border agricultural trade. We handle customs documentation, port coordination, regulatory approvals, and logistics to ensure smooth, timely shipments.

Quality Assurance & Standards

Full compliance with CAMCONTROL, HACCP, ISO food safety, and international quality standards. Our quality management ensures your products meet buyer specifications every time.

Supply Chain Management

Integrated logistics coordination including cold chain management for perishables, secure warehouse solutions, and inventory optimization for maximum freshness and efficiency.

Market Access & Distribution

Strategic connections to international buyers and distribution networks across ASEAN and beyond. We match producers with the right partners to maximize market reach and pricing.

Trade Documentation

Complete management of export/import permits, certificates of origin, phytosanitary certificates, and all required trade paperwork. Avoid delays and compliance issues.

Trade Finance Advisory

Expert guidance on letters of credit, trade insurance, and payment facilitation solutions. Optimize working capital and reduce payment risk in international transactions.
