Cambodia Special Economic Zones: Tax Incentives & Investment Guide

Bodiatrade Team
March 25, 2026
10 min read

Cambodia's Special Economic Zones (SEZs) offer some of the most compelling investment incentives in Southeast Asia, attracting manufacturers, logistics operators, and export-oriented businesses seeking a competitive base within the ASEAN production network. With extended tax holidays, duty-free importation of raw materials and equipment, and streamlined customs procedures, SEZs provide a structured environment where foreign investors can establish operations efficiently while benefiting from preferential regulatory treatment.

Cambodia currently operates more than two dozen SEZs, with several concentrated near the borders with Vietnam and Thailand, and key hubs close to the capital Phnom Penh and the deep-water port at Sihanoukville. These zones have played a significant role in diversifying Cambodia's industrial base beyond the traditional garment sector, attracting electronics assembly, automotive parts, agro-processing, and light manufacturing investments from companies across East Asia, Europe, and North America.

Understanding which SEZ is most suitable for a given investment profile — and how to structure the application for maximum benefit — requires careful analysis of zone location, available incentives, infrastructure quality, and the interaction between SEZ status and the broader Qualified Investment Project framework administered by the Council for the Development of Cambodia.
