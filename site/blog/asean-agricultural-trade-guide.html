ASEAN Agricultural Trade: Regional Market Access

H
Bodiatrade Team
March 25, 2026 10 min read

Cambodia's agricultural sector — encompassing rice, cassava, cashews, mangoes, pepper, and a growing portfolio of processed food products — is increasingly competitive within the ASEAN regional market. The ASEAN Free Trade Area (AFTA) and the broader ASEAN Economic Community framework have significantly reduced tariff barriers on intra-regional trade, creating real market access opportunities for Cambodian producers who can meet importing country quality, phytosanitary, and documentation requirements.

Accessing ASEAN markets is not simply a matter of loading a truck and crossing a border. Each destination country — whether Vietnam, Thailand, Malaysia, Singapore, or Indonesia — maintains its own import procedures, quality standards, labeling requirements, and phytosanitary inspection protocols. Exporters must navigate Cambodia's own export certification system under CAMCONTROL (Cambodia Import Export Inspection and Fraud Repression Directorate-General) alongside the requirements of receiving countries.

Cambodia's position within ASEAN's evolving agricultural trade landscape is strengthened by preferential access arrangements, including the ASEAN Trade in Goods Agreement (ATIGA), bilateral trade agreements, and the Regional Comprehensive Economic Partnership (RCEP), which extends preferential access to major markets including China, Japan, South Korea, and Australia. For exporters who understand and leverage these frameworks, Cambodia's agricultural exports can access a combined regional market of over three billion consumers.
