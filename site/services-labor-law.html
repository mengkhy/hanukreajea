HR Compliance & Employment Law

Cambodia's Labor Law imposes clear obligations on every employer—from the structure of employment contracts and minimum wage requirements to mandatory NSSF contributions and lawful termination procedures. Non-compliance exposes your business to labor disputes, Ministry of Labor inspections, and significant financial liability.

Bodiatrade helps employers build and maintain fully compliant HR frameworks. Whether you are hiring your first employee or auditing an existing workforce of hundreds, our labor law specialists ensure your employment practices, contracts, payroll, and policies meet every requirement of Cambodian law.

Labor Law Services We Provide

End-to-end employment compliance support for businesses of all sizes operating in Cambodia.

Employment Contract Drafting

Legally compliant fixed-duration contracts (FDC) and undetermined-duration contracts (UDC) drafted in accordance with Cambodia's Labor Law. Bilingual Khmer-English versions available for foreign employee agreements.

NSSF Registration & Payroll

National Social Security Fund employer and employee registration, along with ongoing monthly NSSF contribution management. We calculate correct contribution amounts and ensure timely submission each month.

HR Policy Development

Employee handbook, leave policy, overtime policy, and disciplinary procedure documentation aligned with Cambodia's Labor Law requirements. Clear, enforceable policies that protect both employer and employee.

Work Permit Assistance

Work permit and Professional Employment Card (PEC) applications for foreign employees. We manage the full application process with the Ministry of Labor and Vocational Training, including renewals and extensions.

Payroll Compliance Review

Audit of existing payroll for minimum wage compliance, overtime calculation accuracy, annual leave entitlements, public holiday pay, and seniority indemnity obligations under Cambodian law.

Labor Dispute Support

Expert guidance on lawful termination procedures, severance pay and seniority indemnity calculations, and representation during Ministry of Labor conciliation. We help resolve disputes efficiently and protect your business from litigation.
